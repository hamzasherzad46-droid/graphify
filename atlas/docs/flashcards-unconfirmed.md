## Errors found in the imported OpenStax text

Not cited by any card; listed so they can be flagged to readers or reported to OpenStax.

- **13.4 The Peripheral Nervous System** (`s13-4#fs-id1472046`): “the radial nerve from which the axillary nerve branches”. The axillary and radial nerves are both terminal branches of the posterior cord of the brachial plexus; the axillary nerve does not branch from the radial nerve.
- **16.4 The Sensory and Motor Exams** (`s16-4#fs-id2162243`): “the distal head of the ulna on the medial aspect of the elbow”. The head of the ulna is at its distal end, at the wrist; the prominence at the medial elbow is the medial epicondyle of the humerus (the olecranon of the ulna is posterior).
